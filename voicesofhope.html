Founder & Director

Lucy Caroline Kasemeire

Founder & Executive Director

Lucy is a law student at Uganda Christian University, Mukono who started a youth-led initiative established in 2024. We are committed to transforming mental health among young people, equipping communities to end gender-baed violence, and championing the rights of children with disabilities. Inspired by these marginalized groups, I believe young people can be a collective force for change. Your Voice Foundation is thta platform; driven by youth, for youth, building a future of healing, inclusion, and opportunity.

"I believe that when we give young people a platform to speak, heal, and grow — we don't just change individuals, we transform entire communities."
