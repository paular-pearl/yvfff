Testimonials

Voices of Hope & Healing

Every story matters. Here's what our community has to say about the impact of Your Voice Foundation.

"Starting Your Voice Foundation was born out of a deep conviction that every young person deserves to be heard. Seeing our team grow from a small group of passionate friends to a movement impacting hundreds of lives has been the most rewarding journey. When a child at St. Lilian smiles because they feel seen, or a survivor finds the courage to speak — that is why we exist."

Lucy Caroline Kasemeire
Founder & Director

"YVF has become a meaningful part of who i am. Whether through impactful community outreaches or inspiring poetry shows, every initiative reminds me of the power of using our voices for healing and transformation. Being involved in this meaningful work fills me with purpose, knowing we are building safer, more compassionate communities one story, one conversation, and one act of support at a time."

Amaro Hope Jolly
Paperwork and Documentation Personnel, YVF

"The Girl Child Run is more than just an event. It is a heartbeat of Iga-Hub, a powerful initiative where girls are empowered to create reusable sanitary pads and support underprivileged peers. In partnership with organisations like Your Voice Foundation, we continue to uplift and dignify the girl child."

Wandera Andrew

"I have a strong belief that change starts with a few people who have a vision for better circumstances, then later it becomes a fundamental shift to various systems. Your Voice Foundation is an organization by the youth who have a vision for a world that is inclusive and appreciative of all the intersectionality of children , women and the youth. Breaking stereotypes, creating awareness , and resolving problems faced in the society. Change is systemic and I am honored to serve with like minded individuals that believe that it starts now."

Namatovu Rhoda Adyline
Logistics Personnel,YVF

"Mental health is essential to overall well-being it shapes how we think, feel, and connect with others, and it deserves the same attention as physical health. We sincerely thank your foundation for partnering with us on this mental health initiative. Your support and shared commitment made a meaningful impactful. We truly value this collaboration and look forward to working together again."

Owere Louis
Founder, Ngizwe Foundation

"As a someone passionate about writing and mental health advocacy, participating in the Healing Pen Competition was an opportunity to use my pen to speak, use words to break the stigma, give language to the silent world of many and raise awareness in the spectrum of mental health. Writing helps to express the unspoken, to speak the unexpressed and with every story, silent battle told, the voice of awareness becomes louder, the power of stigma crumbles and people realize they are not alone."

Agaba Noel
Participant, Healing Pen writing Competition

"I joined YVF as a university student looking for purpose. Today, I coordinate outreach events and have helped organise three major campaigns. This foundation doesn't just talk about change — it trains young leaders to create it."

Mulungi Ethel Elizabeth
Project Manager, YVF
